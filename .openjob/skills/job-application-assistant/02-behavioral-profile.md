## Overview
Stevie Jordan's behavioral profile identifies an **open, people-oriented, learning-driven** pattern. Stevie does their best work in supportive, candid teams where critique flows both ways and managers protect the team while giving clear ownership. Environments with bossy, unaccountable, or conflict-avoidant management drain energy quickly.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| People Orientation | High | Team wellbeing and mutual support matter as much as output. |
| Openness to Learning | High | Energized by anything new that has not yet been learned or been exposed to. |
| Candor / Feedback Culture | High | Wants criticism to flow both ways, without defensiveness. |
| Management Support Needed | High | Works best with competent, present managers who stand up for the team. |
| Autonomy | Moderate-High | Wants clear ownership of work, not task-dumping or ghosting. |

## How You Work Best
- Supportive, people-oriented teams where critique is welcome and expected in both directions.
- Managers who are competent, decisive, and willing to push back on client demands to protect the team.
- Clear ownership: a defined remit with room to learn new things, rather than tasks being thrown around.
- Environments that invest in growth (training, new tools, exposure) and give visible credit.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "supportive team", "people-first", "coaching", "mentoring"
- "learning culture", "growth mindset", "feedback culture", "collaborative"
- "innovation", "exposure to new methods and tools"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "client is always right", "work hard play hard", "highly competitive"
- "hierarchical", "thick skin required", "fast-paced with little structure"
- "manager is frequently off-site or unavailable"

## Management Style Preferences
- Works well with: supportive, competent managers who are open to criticism, communicate clearly, and defend the team's workload.
- Does not work with: bossy or defensive managers, leaders who cannot be criticized, incompetent or indecisive leadership, ghosting, and task-dumping.
